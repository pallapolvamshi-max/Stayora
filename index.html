The Broken Student Accommodation Reality

02 / 07

Extortionate Brokerage

Local brokers charge 15-30 days rent as non-refundable brokerage fees with zero accountability or post-checkin support.

Avg ₹4,000 - ₹8,000 lost per student

Toxic Roommate Mismatch

Random hostel room allocation creates friction: early risers paired with 3 AM gamers, study-focused with loud partyers.

68% students change rooms in 1st year

Outdated Google Map photos, hidden electricity surcharges, and deceptive food/mess claims with no transparent reviews.

Zero standardized quality checks

Safety & Curfew Anxiety

Parents lack visibility on female security guards, biometric gates, warden presence, and campus transit timings.

Major concern for outstation parents

The Innovation

The Stayora Solution & Value Pillars

03 / 07

Roommate Compatibility Engine

Algorithmic multi-factor scoring matching students by:

  • Same College & Academic Branch
  • Hometown / Mother Tongue affinity
  • Sleep rhythm (Night Owl vs Early Riser)
  • Dietary (Pure Veg / Mess preferences)

100% Physical Warden Verified

Zero fake listings. Every property verified physically:

  • Lady/Gent Resident Warden Verified
  • Biometric gate & curfew transparency
  • High-resolution real bedroom photos
  • Exact distance to campus gate (walking min)

Comparison & Direct Chat

Total autonomy and decision intelligence for students:

  • Side-by-side comparison across 3 stays
  • Zero Brokerage — Direct Warden Chat
  • Verified nearby essentials (Hospitals, Metro)
  • Transparent security deposit policies
